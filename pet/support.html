Need help or found a bug?

Email play@jkn.me with your phone model and Android version. For display problems, a photo of the Glyph Matrix or a screenshot of the in-app display helps a lot. You can also post in the Nothing Community thread.

Getting started

1. Install

Install NonoGotchi from Google Play. Android 14 or newer is required.

2. Hatch your egg

Open the app and give your pet a name. The egg hatches on its own after a while; keep the app installed and let time pass.

3. Put it on the Glyph Matrix

In the app, open Settings and tap Activate NonoGotchi Toy. This opens Nothing's Glyph Toys manager: add NonoGotchi to your active toys. Then cycle through your toys with the Glyph button on the back of the phone until the pet shows up. A long press on the Glyph button makes the pet react.

If the pet does not appear, check that the Glyph Matrix is enabled in Android Settings > Glyph Interface, and that NonoGotchi is in the active toys list, not only the available list.

Compatibility

Which phone is this made for?

The Nothing Phone (3). All graphics are drawn for its 25×25 Glyph Matrix and that is the only device I test on.

I have a Phone (4a) Pro. Why does my pet look rough?

Your matrix is 13×13. The app detects that and downscales the 25×25 picture to fit, so roughly every second pixel is dropped. The pet loses detail and small effects such as tears, hearts or music notes can be hard to read. This is the expected degraded mode, not a bug. The game itself, including care actions, notifications and focus mode, works exactly as on the Phone (3). Touch on the Glyph button is not available on this device.

I have a Phone (1), (2), (2a) or (3a).

Those phones have Glyph light strips, not a matrix, so the pet cannot be drawn on the back. The app still installs and runs with its virtual display on screen. The same applies to any non-Nothing Android phone.

Playing

What do the actions do?

  • Feed raises hunger; Snack appears when the pet is peckish and gives a small happiness bonus. Overfeeding has consequences.
  • Clean removes droppings; Wash appears when cleanliness drops under 50.
  • Play and Pet raise happiness. Playing costs energy.
  • Sleep / Wake: energy recovers while sleeping; a sleeping pet refuses everything else. Shaking the phone wakes it.
  • Medicine is only available when the pet is sick.

My pet refused an action.

That is personality, not a bug. A pet that is asleep, sick, full or simply in a mood will say no. Read its message and try again later.

The pet says it is going to leave.

Neglect builds up when vitals stay low for a long time. Feed, clean and play with it regularly and the warning goes away. If it does leave, you can start over with a new egg from Settings.

Focus mode

How do I start a focus session?

Focus is an adult-only feature. Once your pet has reached the adult stage, a Focus (25 min) button appears on the home screen. Tap it. The pet enters its concentrated pose with a thought bubble on the Glyph Matrix and the button turns into Stop focus with the minutes left. A chip at the top of the home screen shows the remaining time as well.

What happens during a session?

Your pet refuses to play or be petted ("I'm in the zone!") and its happiness decays at half speed. Feeding and cleaning still work.

What do I get for finishing?

When the 25 minutes are up, the session ends by itself: the pet celebrates and gains happiness and health. If you stop early, there is no bonus, just a kind word.

Can I use it from the Glyph side without opening the app?

Yes, when the NonoGotchi toy is active: one shake starts a session. Shake again during a session and the pet will first encourage you to continue; a second shake, or one strong shake, ends it.

Why can't I start a session?

The pet needs to be an adult, awake, not sick, and not too hungry or weak. Take care of it first and the button lights up again.

Weather

How do I set my city?

Settings > Weather: type a city name and confirm with the button on the right of the field. Weather is fetched from Open-Meteo using that city name only, and it is purely cosmetic: rain, snow or sun on the matrix. It does not affect your pet's health.

Notifications

Reminders fire when the pet is hungry, dirty, tired or sick. You can snooze them, honour the system Do Not Disturb, or switch them off entirely in Settings. Actions on a notification (Feed, Clean, ...) apply immediately without opening the app.

Data and privacy

Nothing leaves your phone. See the privacy policy. Uninstalling the app deletes the pet for good; there is no cloud backup by design.
