Privacy policy

NONOGOTCHI
Overview Support & FAQ Privacy policy Open source licenses

Effective date: November 8, 2026

In one sentence: NonoGotchi does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your pet lives entirely on your device. The only network request is an optional weather lookup for a city name you type in yourself.

1. What we do not collect

  • No personal identification information
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No location data: weather uses a city name you enter, never the phone's GPS
  • No usage statistics or analytics
  • No crash reports or diagnostic data
  • No accounts, no login, no user-generated content leaving the device

2. Where your data lives

All NonoGotchi data is stored locally on your phone: the pet's state and history in a local database, your settings in Android's local preferences. Nothing is uploaded to any server and there is no cloud backup or sync. If you uninstall the app, everything is deleted and cannot be recovered. Android's own device backup may include the app data if you have it enabled; that backup is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.

3. Network connections

The app requests the Internet permission for exactly one feature: the optional weather display. When you type a city name in Settings, the app sends that city name to the Open-Meteo geocoding and forecast API to fetch the current weather and show rain, snow or sunshine on the Glyph Matrix. The request contains the city name and nothing else about you. If you never enter a city, the app never connects to the network.

Open-Meteo's privacy terms: open-meteo.com/en/terms.

4. Permissions

  • Internet: weather lookup only (see above)
  • Vibration: haptic feedback when you interact with the pet
  • Notifications: reminders that the pet needs care; can be disabled in Settings or in Android
  • Run at startup: so the pet keeps living and reminders keep working after a reboot
  • Foreground service: keeps the Glyph Matrix display running while the toy is active
  • Glyph interface (Nothing permission): draws the pet on the Glyph Matrix
  • Sensors: accelerometer and gyroscope for shake interactions; readings are processed on the device and never stored

5. Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo: weather data, optional, receives only the city name you typed
  • Nothing Glyph Matrix SDK: draws on the Glyph display; runs locally and collects nothing

There are no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the app.

6. Children

NonoGotchi is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal data at all, no information about children (or anyone else) is ever processed by us.

7. Your rights and your control

Since we hold no data about you, requests for access, correction, deletion or portability have nothing to act on. You keep full control locally: reset the pet from Settings, disable the weather feature to stop all network access, or uninstall the app to erase everything.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address and the change will be mentioned in the app's release notes on Google Play.
