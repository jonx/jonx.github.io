NonoGotchi

VIRTUAL PET FOR THE GLYPH MATRIX
Overview Support & FAQ Privacy policy Open source licenses

A Tamagotchi-style creature that lives on the back of your Nothing Phone. Feed it, clean up after it, play with it, let it sleep - and once it grows up, sit down and focus with it.

NonoGotchi is developed for the Nothing Phone (3) and its 25×25 Glyph Matrix. Every pixel of every animation is drawn for that grid. Other Glyph Matrix phones can run it, but see the compatibility table below before you install.

Get it on Google Play One-time purchase. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.

Which phone do I need?

The short version: Phone (3) is the device this was made for. Phones with a smaller Glyph Matrix get a downscaled picture, which works but looks rough. Phones without a matrix only get the in-app display.

DeviceMatrixExperience
Nothing Phone (3) 25×25 Full The reference device. Everything is designed and tested here.
Nothing Phone (4a) Pro 13×13 Degraded The 25×25 frame is downscaled to 13×13. The pet loses detail, small effects such as tears or music notes become hard to read, and the Glyph button has no touch input. The game itself works normally.
Other Glyph Matrix phones varies Degraded Same downscaling path as above. Untested.
Phone (1), (2), (2a), (3a), other Android phones none App only These phones have light strips or nothing at all, not a matrix. The pet lives on the virtual display inside the app and never appears on the back of the phone.

Android 14 or newer is required on every device. The Glyph part needs the Nothing Glyph Toys system: after installing, add NonoGotchi to your active toys (the app has a button for it in Settings).

What your pet does

  • Grows through five stages: Egg → Baby → Child → Teen → Adult. Each stage has its own look and its own reactions to a shake of the phone.
  • Gets hungry, tired, dirty and occasionally sick. Feed, clean, wash, pet and give medicine from the home screen or straight from a notification.
  • Keeps living while the app is closed. Come back after a day of neglect and it will let you know.
  • Reacts to the weather of the city you type in. Sun, clouds, rain and snow in the app; rain, snow and lightning on the matrix if you turn that on. Cosmetic only.
  • Shows up on the always-on display and answers the Glyph button with a long press.
  • Has a secret. Overfeeding is not recommended.

Focus mode

Once your pet is an adult, a Focus button appears on the home screen. Tap it to start a 25-minute session, Pomodoro style. Your pet sits still on the Glyph Matrix with a little thought bubble, refuses to play, and waits with you. When the timer runs out the session ends on its own and your pet gains happiness and health. Tap Stop focus before the end and the session is cancelled without a reward - no hard feelings, it will say so.

With the Glyph toy active you can also toggle a session by shaking the phone once. If you shake it again mid-session your pet will first try to talk you out of quitting. A strong shake ends the session right away.

Privacy

No data is collected or shared, at all. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, and nothing on this website tracks you either. The only network request the app makes is to fetch the weather for the city you type in, and you can simply not type one. Full privacy policy.
